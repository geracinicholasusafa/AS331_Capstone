Internal heat and thermal control

Internal heat (heater fixed power)
0%

This is the satellite's internal heat, set by you. Let go of the slider to send it. Start low (about 20%) and watch the temperature.

Thermal control: hold temperature
°C

15 to 40 °C. Hold switches closed-loop control on: when the panel goes above this temperature the satellite turns its louvers away from the light, then keeps adjusting the louver opening to hold the panel at this temperature for whatever heater power you set. It does not change the heater.
